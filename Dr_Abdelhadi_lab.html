Communications, Artificial Intelligence, Systems, and Security (CAISS) Lab

Current Dr Abdelhadi Lab Testbed

Dr. Abdelhadi’s lab, Communications, Artificial Intelligence, Systems, and Security (CAISS) Lab, is located in Room 100H of Harrington Hall/ Jodsaas Center building at the University of North Dakota.

Prior Dr Abdelhadi Lab Testbed

Dr. Abdelhadi’s lab, Communications, Artificial Intelligence, Systems, and Security (CAISS) Lab, was located in Room 221 of T2 building at the University of Houston. The lab is equipped with 5 workstations including two computationally powerful workstations dedicated to Deep Learning research. These workstations can execute computationally intensive software codes and store and retrieve big datasets suitable for AI and big data research. Dr. abdelhadi lab is also equipped with state of the art software defined radio (SDR) devices including a massive MIMO testbed.
